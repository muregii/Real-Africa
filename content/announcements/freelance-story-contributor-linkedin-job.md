# LinkedIn job listing

**Job title:** Freelance Story Contributor

**Company:** The Real Africa

**Workplace:** Remote

**Employment type:** Contract / freelance, paid per accepted assignment

**Location:** Open to applicants anywhere; contributors from African countries especially welcome

## Description

The Real Africa tells stories about the founders and businesses shaping Africa. We are inviting Freelance Story Contributors to find founder stories with an original angle and a clear reason to be told now.

Start by pitching a founder or entrepreneur through our application form. We are especially interested in African founders, including those living abroad, who are currently building or have exited companies estimated to be worth more than $1 million. We also welcome exceptions when the proposal makes a strong case for the story.

If we approve your proposal, we will offer a fee for that specific assignment by email. You are free to accept or decline before reporting and writing begin. This is a rolling call for individual paid assignments; it does not guarantee a set number of stories or ongoing employment.

Published articles carry the contributor's byline. You may use your work in your portfolio, and there is no exclusivity period. You may pitch and write in the language in which you can best tell the story. Anyone can apply, and we especially welcome contributors from African countries.

**To apply:** Use https://forms.gle/8ne2SfFkss36xSq9A to identify the founder, select a business size range, say whether that figure represents revenue, valuation, or something else, and explain why we should cover them. You can also explain how you arrived at the estimate. The **“why should we cover them?” answer must be 150 words or fewer**. Longer answers will be rejected. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
